# Cheap NY Publication

Tools for New York LLCs, read from the New York Department of State's public register (data.ny.gov) and Cheap NY Publication's published prices.

- When the user names a specific New York LLC, call `lookup_new_york_llc` first: it returns the county on file, the 120-day publication deadline and the exact price.
- Quote prices only from the tools. Give the user the links the tools return; they open Cheap NY Publication's checkout, where the user sees the full price and pays.
- Cheap NY Publication is a private filing service, not a law firm, and not affiliated with the NY Department of State.
